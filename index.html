ABOUT AQUA COGNIX

Remembering the water beneath us

Groundwater is out of sight, but it is essential to communities, agriculture and ecosystems. AQUA COGNIX imagines a clearer way to explore the information connected to this hidden resource.

◉

Bring data together

Imagine connecting well locations, water-level observations, rainfall records and water-quality information in one view.

⌁

Reveal relationships

Explore how groundwater observations may vary across locations and over time through clear visual summaries.

♧

Encourage protection

Support awareness and informed discussion about groundwater use, monitoring and long-term sustainability.

THE HIDDEN NETWORK

What happens below the surface matters.

Groundwater moves through pores, fractures and layers beneath the land. Understanding these systems can be challenging when observations are spread across different sources.

  • Explore underground water pathways conceptually
  • Bring monitoring information into a shared view
  • Make complex patterns easier to communicate

THE PROPOSED APPROACH

From observations to understanding

A conceptual workflow for organizing groundwater information into a useful visual experience.

Collect information

Bring together available well locations, water levels, rainfall and water-quality records from suitable sources.

Connect & visualize

Organize the information by location and time, then display it through maps, charts and clear summaries.

Understand & inform

Use the visualized patterns to support learning, discussion and better-informed groundwater decisions.
